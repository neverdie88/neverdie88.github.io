Privacy Policy for Guitar Chord Visualizer

Last updated: 2025-12-04

Application ID: com.ductnguy.guitarchordvisualizer

This Privacy Policy describes how Guitar Chord Visualizer (“the App”, “we”, “our”, “us”) handles information when you use the Android application.

Summary (plain language)

  • We do not collect personal information on our own servers.
  • The App uses third-party services:
    • Google AdMob for displaying ads.
    • Google Play Billing for processing the one‑time “Remove Ads” purchase.
  • On-device storage is used only for App preferences and bookmarks (e.g., “ads removed” flag, saved chord bookmarks). You can clear this by uninstalling or clearing the App’s storage.
  • The App requires internet access to load SoundFonts and to serve ads.
  • The App is not directed to children under 13.

Information We Process

1) Information you provide

The App does not require sign‑in or user registration. You may interact with settings and bookmarks; these remain on your device.

2) On-device storage (local)

The App stores a small amount of data locally on your device, for example:

  • ads_removed_v1: a flag indicating that you purchased “Remove Ads”.
  • Bookmarks and UI preferences (e.g., last viewed position on the fretboard, selected instrument).

This data is stored on-device (e.g., localStorage or app storage). Removing the App or clearing its data resets this information.

3) Third‑party services

We integrate with the following:

Google AdMob (Ads)

  • Purpose: display banner ads and measure ad performance.
  • Data handled by Google may include device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID), IP address, app usage data and interaction with ads. Google may use data for ad personalization depending on your device settings.
  • Provider policies:
    • Google Privacy Policy
    • About Ads & Technologies
    • AdMob Policy

Google Play Billing (In‑App Purchase)

  • Purpose: process non‑consumable purchase “Remove Ads.”
  • Payment credentials are handled by Google; we do not receive or store your full payment information. We receive purchase status necessary to unlock the feature.
  • Google Privacy Policy

We do not control the data practices of these third parties. Please review their privacy and policy pages above.

Permissions

  • Internet access: required for:
    • Downloading SoundFonts (instrument sounds) from a CDN used by the App.
    • Loading and showing Google AdMob ads.
    • Communicating with Google Play services for billing.

The App does not request microphone, camera, contacts, or location permissions.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

If you intend to use the App with an audience including children, ensure AdMob is configured for families and complies with Google Play’s Families policies. For more details, see: https://play.google.com/about/families/

Data Retention and Deletion

  • On-device data (bookmarks, preferences, “ads removed” flag) remains until you clear App storage or uninstall the App.
  • Purchase entitlements are tied to your Google account via Google Play Billing and can be restored on re‑install using the “Restore Purchases” action (if provided).

Security

We implement reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature of the App; however, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

International Transfers

Third‑party services (e.g., Google AdMob) may process data on servers located in various countries. See Google’s privacy disclosures for details.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the contact email provided on the App’s Google Play store listing (Developer contact).

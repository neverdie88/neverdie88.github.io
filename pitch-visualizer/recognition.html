About sheet recognition

Your photo is processed in this browser. It is never uploaded. The first conversion downloads about 100 MB of recognition models, plus the WebAssembly runtime. The browser caches the models when storage is available; your photos and scores are kept in memory until you close or reload the page. Use Save MusicXML to keep a score.

This is experimental recognition for clear printed music. Use a flat, evenly lit page photographed straight on. Staff lines are read from top to bottom as one instrument. For piano or ensemble scores, choose a single staff line. Handwriting, curved pages, severe perspective, and dense notation may not work. Time signatures are estimated; notes, accidentals, rhythm, ornaments, and ties need review. Use Edit sheet to correct the recognized notes, or Open MusicXML for an existing score.

Free play shows your pitch trace and played notes without a score. Choose treble or bass staff and a major or minor scale. Switching to Follow sheet keeps your loaded score and pauses practice.

Practice with sheet places the current note or chord in the middle, with upcoming notes to the right and your pitch traces to the left. Play piano chords together, or their tones one at a time in any order. Matched tones fade, and completing the chord moves the next group into the middle. Hold briefly and release repeated notes or chords before playing again. Practice follows one selected staff in written order, skips rests and grace notes, and does not grade rhythm. Transposing parts use sounding pitch. Use View full sheet to review the notation. Edit sheet opens a staff composer: choose a note length, tap to write, drag pitches, stack chord tones, or erase. Play preview listens to the selected staff and voice. Select a note or chord, then change its length, dots or accidental in the palette. The sheet wraps into rows. Staff and Scale are above the sheet; title and time are in Sheet settings. Select multiple highlights groups for Delete selected, with one Undo restoring the deletion. Undo/Redo, Apply and Cancel keep changes under your control. Save MusicXML keeps the applied edits on your device. New sheet starts a blank score.

Chord listening runs locally using your device's microphone. Audio is never uploaded or saved. It loads a 230 KB model and the shared WebAssembly runtime. Recognition is experimental: quiet notes, dense chords, octave doubling, background noise, and sustain-pedal overlap can hide tones. If a tone is missed, play it separately. Single-note targets use the existing pitch detector.

Recognition source and licenses

Recognition uses HOMR checkpoint 426, based on Polyphonic-TrOMR. The models and browser adapters use AGPL-3.0. The adapters were added on 17 September 2026. Their complete editable source is served with the app; no compilation is needed:

  • Image preprocessing, staff detection, and model inference
  • Recognized symbols to MusicXML
  • Worker, model loading, and caching
  • Model vocabulary and model file hashes
  • Photo and camera controls, score parsing and note matching, practice staff and trace, display and practice controls, and styles. The page source of the visualizer contains its microphone app.

The model protocol and vocabulary are from HOMR commit 5e51b434b3149286dae1448603527923b62ef9f2. The weights are from its ONNX checkpoint release, checkpoint identifier 426-b6fd20809a8dcaf10dfd39a4ca4f64c6f056e644.

ONNX Runtime Web 1.23.2 runs the models using WebAssembly (MIT license). OpenSheetMusicDisplay 2.1.2 draws the notation (BSD-3-Clause license).

Chord listening uses Basic Pitch, Copyright Spotify AB, under the Apache-2.0 license. The ICASSP 2022 ONNX model is pinned to commit fa5997af0a8210982619003269994a1be25eddf3; see its source and hash. The live browser adapters are audio processing and inference, the worker, and the live listener.

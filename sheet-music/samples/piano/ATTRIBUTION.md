# Salamander Grand Piano V3

Piano recordings by **Alexander Holm**, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Original recordings: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
MP3 conversion and distribution: https://github.com/darosh/samples-piano-mp3
Package: `@audio-samples/piano-mp3-velocity6@1.0.5`.

These are the thirty unmodified MP3 files from velocity layer 6 of that package.
The source recordings were converted to MP3 upstream. Playback changes pitch and
applies a release envelope in the browser; the bundled files are unchanged.
See `manifest.json` for provenance, package integrity and individual SHA-256 hashes.

This compact bank provides one recorded velocity layer. It does not reproduce
all sixteen velocity layers, pedal noises or string resonances of the full bank.

# Little Table credits

Little Table uses **Phaser 3.90.0** by Richard Davey and Phaser Studio Inc.
The bundled engine is distributed under the [MIT license](licenses/phaser-MIT.txt).

The café illustrations, Blender clay artwork, game dialogue, background music,
and action motifs were created for Little Table. Blender source scenes and
generation scripts are retained with the development project.

Customer speech was generated locally with Kokoro-82M and kokoro-onnx.
Only the generated MP3 recordings are included in the playable game; no model
weights, voice tensors, or speech service are needed during play. See the
[voice provenance note](audio/kokoro/NOTICE.md) and
[generation manifest](audio/kokoro/manifest.json).
